Label: Client Operations Platform

Ask any brokerage operations manager where the day disappears, and you'll hear the same answers. Chasing missing ID documents. Matching deposits to the right client. Working out which introducer referred whom. Creating trading accounts by hand. Pulling numbers together for the weekly meeting.

A proper forex CRM takes that scattered work and puts it in one place. Every prospect, every verification, every payment and every trading account sits in a single system linked straight to your MT4 or MT5 server, so your staff stop juggling tabs and start actually serving clients.

General sales CRMs were designed for tracking deals and phone calls. They have no idea what a margin account is, how a withdrawal request should be checked, or why an introducing broker expects a rebate on every lot traded.

A CRM built for brokers handles all of that. It manages prospects and sales activity, yes, but it also runs onboarding, identity checks, funding, trading account creation, partner commissions and reporting. Many brokers think of it as their back office and their client portal rolled together, since clients log in to it too, to upload documents, deposit money and open new accounts.

What Is a Forex CRM?

* Newly launched brokers who need a client lifecycle in place before the first sign-up

* Growing brokerages whose spreadsheets and email threads have stopped scaling

* Firms replacing an older CRM that never integrated properly with their trading server

* Businesses running large introducing broker or affiliate networks

* Brokers with sales teams across several regions and languages

Who Uses It

Rather than listing features in a random pile, here they are in the order a client experiences them.

Stage 1: Acquire & Convert

Lead Management

Prospects arrive from your website, landing pages, partners and campaigns, and every one of them is captured automatically with its source recorded. Sales staff see who to call next, log conversations, set follow-ups and move leads through custom stages. Managers can reassign leads by region, language or workload.

Campaign and Source Tracking

Find out which channels deliver clients who actually fund, rather than just sign up. That insight helps marketing spend go where it earns something back.

Stage 2: Onboard & Verify

Client Registration

A branded sign-up flow collects the details you need, with forms that can change by jurisdiction or account type.

KYC Processing

Clients upload identity and address documents through the portal, or verify through an automated identity check provider you've connected. Your compliance team reviews submissions in a queue, approves, rejects or requests more information, and every decision is stamped with who made it and when.

Client Management

Each client gets a single profile holding personal details, documents, verification status, linked accounts, transaction history, notes and communication records. Anyone with the right permission can see the full picture in seconds.

Stage 3: Fund & Operate

Deposits and Withdrawals

Clients fund through whichever payment methods you've integrated. Deposits credit their trading account once confirmed. Withdrawal requests flow into an approval queue where your finance team checks them against your policies before releasing funds.

Trading Account Management

Once verified, clients can open live or demo accounts themselves, choosing from the account types you offer. The CRM creates them on MT4 or MT5 instantly, places them in the correct group and sends login details. Internal transfers between accounts, leverage change requests and account archiving all happen here too.

Notifications

Automated emails and in-portal alerts keep clients informed. Document approved, deposit received, withdrawal processed, margin level warning. Templates can be edited and translated.

Stage 4: Grow & Retain

IB Management

Introducing brokers get their own portal area with referral links, client lists and earnings dashboards. You can set up multi-level structures where sub-partners sit under master partners.

Commissions and Rebates

Rebate plans can be per lot, per trade, a share of spread or a fixed amount per funded client. The CRM calculates payouts from actual trading data and shows partners exactly what they've earned and why.

Reporting

Funded accounts, net deposits, trading volume, IB performance, sales team conversion, KYC backlog and more. Reports can be filtered by date, region, account type or staff member, then exported for your accounting or board meetings.

Across Every Stage: Role-Based Access

Sales sees leads. Compliance sees documents. Finance sees payments. Dealing sees trading data. Each user gets precisely the access their job needs, and an audit log records every action taken inside the system.

Features Organised Around How a Brokerage Actually Works

The value of a broker CRM comes almost entirely from how well it links to everything around it.

MT4 and MT5 Connection

The CRM talks to your trading server through manager API access. Accounts are created, balances adjusted after deposits and withdrawals, groups assigned and trading data pulled in for reporting and commission calculation. No retyping between systems.

Payment Provider Integration

Card processors, bank transfer services, local payment methods and crypto gateways can be plugged in. Payment confirmations arrive automatically, so deposits credit without someone checking a bank statement first.

KYC Provider Integration

Automated identity verification, liveness checks and sanctions screening services can be linked, depending on which providers you use and what they support. Results flow straight onto the client profile.

Website and Other Systems

Registration forms on your website feed the CRM directly. Marketing tools, help desk software, telephony and accounting systems can connect through APIs and webhooks where needed.

MT5, Payment and KYC Integration

Client → Broker Website → Forex CRM (KYC, Payments, IB) → MT5 / MT4 Server → Liquidity Bridge → Liquidity Providers

A prospect registers on your website and lands in the CRM. They're verified through the KYC flow, fund through an integrated payment provider and receive a trading account generated on your MT5 or MT4 server. Their trades route out through your bridge to liquidity, while trading data flows back into the CRM to drive reports and partner commissions.

Who Handles What

How is a forex CRM different from a regular CRM?

A regular CRM tracks sales. A broker CRM also handles onboarding, verification, funding, trading account creation, partner commissions and a client portal, all linked to your trading server.

Is a forex CRM the same as a back office?

They overlap heavily. Many brokers use the CRM as their main back office, covering clients, payments, accounts and reporting in one system.

Does it work with both MT4 and MT5?

Yes. It connects to either platform, or both at once if you run them side by side.

Can clients manage their own accounts?

Yes. Through the client portal they can upload documents, deposit, request withdrawals, open new trading accounts and transfer funds between them.

Can you connect the payment and KYC providers we already use?

In most cases, yes. We review each provider's integration options during design and confirm what's possible before build starts.

How does IB commission tracking work?

You set the rebate rules. The CRM pulls trading data from your server, calculates what each partner has earned across every level of your structure and displays it in their portal.

Can we move our data from an old CRM?

Yes. We plan the migration, map fields between systems, run trial imports and verify records before switching over.

Does the CRM make us compliant?

No software can do that on its own. Our forex CRM gives your compliance team the tools, records and audit trail to apply your policies, but the obligations remain with your regulated business.
