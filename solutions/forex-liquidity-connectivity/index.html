01 / Liquidity & Connectivity

What Is Forex Liquidity?

Put simply, liquidity is the availability of buyers and sellers ready to trade at a given price. For a brokerage, it usually arrives through a liquidity provider. That might be a bank, a prime broker, a prime-of-prime firm or a non-bank market maker, each streaming bid and ask prices and accepting orders against them.

Your clients never deal with those institutions directly. They trade on your platform. Your platform, in turn, needs a reliable technical route to the provider so prices come in accurately and orders go out quickly. That route is what we mean by connectivity, and it's where a surprising number of brokerage problems begin.

03 / Liquidity & Connectivity

How Connectivity Works

Here's the journey of a single trade, in plain terms.

A client clicks buy on EUR/USD. Their order lands on your MT4 or MT5 server. Depending on your rules, the server either fills it internally or passes it onward through a connector, which might be a bridge, a gateway plugin or a direct FIX session. The connector translates the order into a format your provider understands and sends it across. The provider fills it and returns a confirmation. That confirmation travels back along the same path, the client's position appears on screen, and your reporting records what happened.

All of that typically takes a fraction of a second. Each hop, though, is a spot where a wrong setting can cause trouble.

What We Deliver

01

Liquidity Provider Integration

Before touching any configuration, we gather your provider's technical documentation. Supported protocols, session schedules, instrument lists, minimum and maximum order sizes, permitted order types, and whatever conformance testing they insist on. We flag mismatches early, while they're still cheap to resolve.

02

Liquidity Bridge Setup

A bridge is middleware that sits between your server and one or more providers. It typically handles markups, routing logic, risk-based splitting of flow and multi-source pricing. We install it, configure it to your instructions and connect it at both ends. This is especially useful if you plan to grow beyond one provider.

03

MT5 Gateway Configuration

Where a provider offers a native MT5 gateway, it can be a lighter, more direct option with fewer moving pieces. Fine for straightforward setups with a single source and simple routing. We'll tell you honestly whether a gateway is enough or whether a bridge would serve you better.

04

FIX API Connectivity

FIX, short for Financial Information eXchange, is the protocol institutions use to swap price and order messages. Most bridges and many gateways speak FIX under the hood. We establish and certify these sessions, sort out network routing, credentials and heartbeats, and make sure reconnection behaves sensibly after a drop.

05

Liquidity Aggregation

With two or more providers, aggregation merges their price streams into a single book. Orders can then route by best price, by fixed allocation or by whatever logic you specify. It can tighten what clients see and spread your dependence. It also introduces extra settings to manage, which we'll explain upfront.

06

MT4 and MT5 Integration

Whichever platform you run, or both, we link it with your chosen connector, aligning account groups, execution modes and the way fills are reported back. Brokers running MT4 and MT5 in parallel can share a single bridge, so pricing stays consistent across the two.

07

Symbol Mapping

Providers and platforms rarely name or size instruments identically. One might list gold in ounces, another in lots of a hundred. Suffixes differ. Decimal places differ. Session hours differ. We line up every instrument manually and verify contract size, precision, trading hours and margin currency for each. Sloppy mapping is a classic source of costly mistakes, so this gets real attention.

08

Order Routing

You decide your execution model. Pass everything to the market, keep some flow internally, or use a hybrid split based on account group, trade size or client profile. We translate those decisions into working rules, test them and document them so your dealing team knows exactly what's configured.

09

Monitoring

After go-live, we track feed freshness, session status, fill rates, rejection counts and round-trip timing to each provider. Unusual patterns trigger alerts for our engineers, and you get a clear explanation of what happened if something needs investigating.

09 / Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide liquidity directly?+

No. We're a technology firm. We connect your platform to providers you hold agreements with.

Do I need a bridge, or will an MT5 gateway do?+

For one provider and uncomplicated routing, a gateway is often enough. Multiple sources, aggregation, markups or hybrid execution usually call for a bridge. We'll assess your plans and recommend accordingly.

What is FIX API in forex?+

It's the messaging standard used across institutional trading to stream prices and transmit orders. Your bridge or gateway typically communicates with providers through FIX sessions that we set up and certify.

How does liquidity aggregation help a broker?+

By combining prices from several providers, aggregation can offer clients tighter quotes and reduce reliance on any single source. It requires more configuration and monitoring, which we handle.

How long does it take to connect a provider?+

Timelines vary with the connection method, the number of instruments and how quickly the provider schedules certification. Provider-side steps frequently take the longest. We'll estimate after reviewing their documentation.

Can you fix problems in an existing liquidity setup?+

Yes. We audit current mappings, routing rules and connector settings, identify what's causing the issues and correct them, then document the cleaned-up configuration.

Can MT4 and MT5 share the same liquidity?+

Yes. A single bridge can serve both platforms, keeping pricing aligned while you run them side by side.

Will you guarantee my spreads or execution?+

No. Pricing and fills come from your providers and the market. Our job is to make sure the technology carrying your forex liquidity is accurate, stable and well monitored.
