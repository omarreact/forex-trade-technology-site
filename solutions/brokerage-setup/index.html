Who This Is For

Mostly, people at the very start. Or close to it.

That could be a founder who wants to start a forex brokerage and has nothing technical in place yet. Sometimes it's a group opening a second entity for a new region, or a fintech business stepping into trading services for the first time.

You don't have to show up with everything figured out. A lot of our clients arrive with a business model, a target market and a rough idea of timing. We help fill in the rest.

Already running a brokerage and just need parts of it fixed or replaced? Our maintenance, hosting and integration services will probably suit you better.

What Goes Into a Technology-Led Launch

Every project gets scoped around your model, so no two look identical. That said, most launches cover the same core layers.

01

Choosing and Configuring the Platform

First question: full MT5 deployment or white label? A white label usually costs less and gets you live sooner. A full deployment gives you more control. We'll help you weigh it up against your budget and where you want to be in two or three years.

Once that's decided, we configure the platform properly. Trading groups, account types, symbol specs, leverage and margin rules, and manager access for your dealing and support teams.

02

Servers and Infrastructure

Your platform needs somewhere to live. We set up hosting in a region that makes sense for your clients and your liquidity, lock down access, schedule backups and get monitoring running before launch day. A backup server and a recovery plan are built in from the start, because adding them later is always harder.

03

Connecting Liquidity

Depending on what your providers support and how you plan to execute, we'll connect through a bridge, an MT5 gateway or a direct FIX connection. Symbols get mapped. Price feeds get checked. Orders route the way your risk policy says they should.

04

CRM, KYC and Payments

This is the bit your clients actually notice. Registration, identity checks, account creation, deposits and withdrawals all have to work together, and they all have to talk to the trading platform. We connect your CRM, KYC provider and payment providers so a new client goes from sign-up to a funded account in one smooth flow.

05

Testing Before Anyone Goes Live

We test everything against real scenarios. Price feeds, order execution, rejects, margin calls, onboarding, deposits, withdrawals, reporting. Finding a problem at this stage costs very little. Finding it after your first hundred clients arrive is a different story.

06

Support After Launch

The first few weeks are when small issues tend to pop up. We keep a close eye on things during that period, tweak settings as real trading comes in, and then move you onto an ongoing support plan.

How the Setup Process Works

There's a reason we do things in this order. Each stage relies on the one before it, and jumping ahead nearly always means redoing work.

01

Scoping

We get to know the business. Your target markets, account types, expected client numbers, execution model and any providers you've already picked. By the end, we both know exactly what's being built.

02

Stack Design

Then we draw up the technical blueprint. Platform, hosting region, liquidity method, CRM, KYC and payment providers, and how they all connect. You see the full plan and timeline before any build work begins.

03

Build and Configuration

Servers get provisioned, the platform gets configured, liquidity gets connected. Your trading environment starts to take real shape here.

04

Integration

CRM, KYC and payment systems are linked to the platform and to each other. We also chase your third-party providers for credentials, sandbox access and technical details. Honestly, this is where most launches get delayed when nobody owns it.

05

User Acceptance Testing

Your team and ours run through every client and back-office workflow in a test environment. Anything that breaks gets fixed and retested. We sign off together.

06

Go-Live and Early Support

Launch day happens with monitoring switched on and our engineers close by. After the first stretch, we agree on an ongoing support plan that fits how you operate.

How It All Fits Together

Here's the stack in a single line:

  1. 01Client
  2. 02Website & Client Portal
  3. 03CRM, KYC & Payments
  4. 04MT5 Trading Server
  5. 05Bridge / Gateway / FIX
  6. 06Liquidity Providers
  7. 07Reporting & Back Office

A client signs up on your website. The CRM records them, the KYC provider verifies them, the payment provider handles their deposit. Their trading account opens on MT5. When they trade, orders travel through your connectivity layer to liquidity, and the results flow back into your reports.

If one link is set up badly, everything downstream feels it. So we build and test the whole chain as one system.

Launches go smoother when everyone knows who owns which piece. Usually it splits like this.

08 / Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a forex brokerage setup take?+

That depends on scope, and on how fast third-party providers respond. A white label with standard integrations moves quicker than a full MT5 deployment with several payment providers and multiple liquidity connections. After scoping, we'll give you a timeline based on what you actually need.

Do I need a licence before the technology work starts?+

Not always. Plenty of clients start building while their licence application is still in progress, so everything's ready once approval comes through. Whether you need a licence, and which one, depends on your jurisdiction and business model. A regulatory advisor is the right person for that question.

Full MT5 setup or white label. Which one should I pick?+

White label is generally cheaper upfront and faster to launch. A full deployment gives you more control over configuration and infrastructure. We'll go through the trade-offs with you during scoping, based on your budget and growth plans.

Can you work with providers I've already chosen?+

Yes. If you've already signed with a liquidity provider, KYC service or payment provider, we build around them. Haven't chosen yet? We can walk you through the technical points worth comparing.

What happens if something goes wrong after launch?+

During the early support period, our team watches your environment closely and responds to issues directly. After that, you move onto ongoing maintenance covering monitoring, configuration changes and incident response.

Can I start small and add more later?+

Yes, and it's often the smarter way to go. We build the stack so extra liquidity providers, payment methods, a mobile app or new regions can be added later without rebuilding what's already there.

What does it cost to start a forex brokerage?+

Technology costs depend on your platform choice, hosting, the number of integrations and the level of support you want. Licensing, legal, banking and marketing come on top of that and vary a lot by jurisdiction. Once we understand your scope, we'll give you a clear breakdown of the technology side of your forex brokerage setup.
