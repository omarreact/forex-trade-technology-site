01 / MT5 White Label

What an MT5 White Label Actually Means

There's a fair bit of confusion around this term, so let's keep it simple.

MT5 runs on a server operated under a licence from the platform developer. With a full licence, you own and run that server yourself. That gives you the most control, but it also comes with a large upfront cost and a lot of technical responsibility.

With a white label, your brokerage operates within an existing licensed MT5 environment. You get your own branded setup, your own client groups and manager access to run your business day to day. The core server sits under the licence holder's arrangement rather than yours.

For most new brokers, that trade-off makes sense. Lower startup costs, faster time to market, and you can always look at moving to a full deployment later as volumes grow.

One important point. A white label is a technology arrangement. It isn't a financial licence and it doesn't give you permission to offer trading services anywhere. That part is covered further down.

02 / MT5 White Label

Who It Suits

A white label MT5 setup tends to work well for:

  • New brokers who want to launch on MT5 without committing to a full server licence on day one
  • Introducing brokers ready to move clients under their own brand
  • Regional brokerages testing a new market before investing in bigger infrastructure
  • Businesses currently on MT4 who want to move to MT5 without a heavy upfront spend

If you already process high volumes, need deep control over server-level settings or plan to run several entities from one environment, a full deployment might suit you better. We're happy to talk through both.

09 / Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between an MT5 white label and a full licence?+

With a full licence, you own and run your own MT5 server, which means higher costs and more control. With a white label, you operate inside an existing licensed environment under your own brand. It's cheaper and quicker to launch, with somewhat less server-level control.

How is a white label different from a grey label?+

The terms get used loosely across the industry. Generally, a white label gives you more branding and configuration control, while a grey label sits more directly under another provider's infrastructure and arrangement. We'll explain exactly what you get with any setup before you commit.

Will my clients see my brand or someone else's?+

Your clients interact with your brand through the terminals, client portal and communications. What's visible depends on the specific arrangement, and we'll go through that clearly during scoping.

Can I set my own spreads, leverage and account types?+

Yes, within the limits of your liquidity setup and the rules that apply in your jurisdiction. You can run several account types with different conditions.

Can I connect my own liquidity provider?+

Usually, yes. We connect your chosen provider through a bridge, gateway or FIX, depending on what they support.

How long does an MT5 broker setup take with a white label?+

It depends on your account structure, the number of integrations and how quickly your third-party providers respond. White labels generally launch faster than full deployments. We'll give you a realistic timeline after scoping.

Can I move from a white label to a full MT5 server later?+

Yes. Many brokers start on a white label and move to their own server as they grow. We plan the configuration so that migration, when it comes, is as straightforward as possible.

Does a white label include a licence to operate as a broker?+

No. An MT5 white label covers the technology only. Financial licensing and regulatory approval are separate, and they remain your responsibility.
