From First Call to Go-Live: How a Launch Comes Together

A brokerage launch has a lot of moving parts. Here's the order we work in, and why.

01

Discovery

We start with your business model. Target markets, account types, expected volumes, the providers you already have. It saves a lot of rework later.

02

Architecture

Next we map the stack: which platform, where it's hosted, how liquidity connects and which systems need to sync with what.

Platform, bridge, CRM, KYC and payment providers get connected and configured. This is usually the longest stage, and the one most worth getting right.

04

Testing

Pricing, order flow, rejects, deposits, onboarding. We test the real workflows end to end before a single live client touches them.

05

Go-Live

Launch happens with monitoring already running and a clear plan for the first few days.

06

Managed Support

After launch, we stay on. Updates, configuration changes, incident response and the small adjustments every new brokerage needs in its first months.

How Your Trading Stack Connects

It helps to see the whole thing in one line:

Each arrow is a connection that has to be configured, tested and watched. A client places a trade on your platform. The order passes through the bridge or gateway to your liquidity, comes back with an execution, and then the result has to show up correctly in your reporting and CRM. When one link is set up badly, it rarely announces itself. It shows up later as a pricing gap, a stuck withdrawal or a report that doesn't add up.

That's why we treat the stack as one system rather than a collection of separate purchases.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a forex brokerage technology provider actually do?+

We supply and connect the technical systems a brokerage runs on. That includes the trading platform, servers, liquidity connectivity, CRM, KYC and payment integrations, along with ongoing support. We don't act as a broker, and we don't give investment or regulatory advice.

Can you help us launch a brokerage from scratch?+

Yes. Our brokerage setup service covers platform selection, hosting, liquidity connectivity, CRM and KYC integration, testing and go-live. Licensing and regulatory approval remain your responsibility, and requirements vary by jurisdiction.

Do you only work with MT5?+

MT5 is our main focus for new deployments, but we also maintain MT4 environments and help brokers plan migrations from MT4 to MT5.

What's the difference between a bridge, a gateway and a FIX connection?+

All three connect your trading platform to liquidity. They differ in how orders are routed, how much control you have and how they're maintained. Which one suits you depends on your providers and execution model, and we'll walk you through the options during discovery.

Can you take over support of an existing MT5 setup?+

Yes. We start with a technical review of your current configuration, hosting and integrations, then agree on a maintenance plan covering monitoring, upgrades and incident response.

Do you handle KYC and AML compliance for us?+

We handle the technical integration: connecting verification providers, building the approval workflow and syncing results to your CRM. Your compliance obligations stay with your regulated business.

Can we choose only the services we need?+

Absolutely. Our forex brokerage technology is modular. Some clients come to us only for hosting or liquidity connectivity, while others want the full stack.
